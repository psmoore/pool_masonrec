The pool

The Aquatic and Fitness Center competition pool is a 50-metre tank with two movable bulkheads. It sets up as 20 lanes short course (25 yd) or 8 lanes long course (50 m), and carries varsity swimming and diving, Mason club and Masters programmes, and open recreational swimming through the day.

Pool schedule

Live

Lane by lane, hour by hour. Move between days with the arrows, and tap a programme in the filter to see only that. On a phone the same schedule opens as a list.

What this replaces

  • The schedule is currently a PDF, and its filename tells the story: Comp-Pool-No-Trinity_Master-26-27-3.pdf — revision three of a master document for the year, re-exported and re-uploaded every time a practice moves.
  • A PDF cannot say “today”. A swimmer has to download it, find the right day, and hope the copy on the site is the current one. The calendar opens on today by default.
  • Varsity in lanes 1–14 while diving has 17–20, open swim in 15–16 — the lane split is the whole point of the document, and it survives here rather than being flattened to “pool busy”.
  • Short course and long course are two configurations of the same water, so the same calendar shows either without a second document.
